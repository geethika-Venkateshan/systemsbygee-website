My story

I started young. I was 21, still in my final year of university, already building websites and apps for real clients.

The tech VA work started as a side thing, freelance gigs alongside the dev work. But something kept happening. Clients did not want to let me go.

Not because I ticked boxes fast. Because I actually solved things. A broken automation, a system held together with tape, a process nobody had time to fix. I would dig in and sort it. And I never ghosted. Ever. In a space where half the tech VAs disappear mid-project, just showing up consistently turned out to be rare.

So clients kept pulling me deeper into their businesses. "Can you fix this?" became "Can you figure out why this keeps breaking?" became "Can you just own this whole system?"

That is how the work evolved on its own. From tech VA, to building systems, to GoHighLevel expert and systems strategist.

And here is what makes me different, then and now. I do not work through your to-do list. I work alongside your business. I fix the system, then I strategize the next one. I am thinking about what breaks next, not just what you asked me to do today.

You did not build your business to become its full-time admin. You built it to lead. I build the backend that lets you do exactly that.
